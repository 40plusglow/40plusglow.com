Our Story

More than a brand. A growing wellness movement.

40PlusGlow was created to inspire people to embrace health, confidence, self-care and purposeful living at every stage of life.

Through trusted wellness information, encouraging community content, premium products and in-person experiences, our goal is to make healthier living feel welcoming, achievable and empowering.

We believe your age does not define your potential. You can continue learning, growing, thriving and glowing.
