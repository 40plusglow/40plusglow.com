Who We Are

More than products. A wellness movement.

40PlusGlow exists to empower people to prioritize their health, embrace confidence and create intentional self-care habits at every stage of life.

We combine wellness education, premium self-care products, uplifting content and community experiences to help people feel healthier, stronger and beautifully empowered.

Wellness Education Practical information that supports healthier choices.
Intentional Self-Care Products and rituals designed to help you slow down and glow.
Confidence Encouraging people to feel good in every season of life.
Community Connecting people through content, events and shared wellness goals.
