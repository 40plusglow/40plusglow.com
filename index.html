WHO WE ARE

More Than Products. A Health & Wellness Movement.

40PlusGlow was created from a simple belief: wellness should be welcoming, empowering, and accessible to everyone. We envisioned more than a place to shop—we set out to create a trusted wellness destination where people could discover premium self-care products, embrace healthier habits, and find encouragement at every stage of their wellness journey.

What began as a passion for intentional self-care has grown into a wellness brand dedicated to inspiring confidence, balance, and healthy living. Every product we offer, every educational resource we share, and every connection we make reflects our commitment to helping people nurture their mind, body, and soul.

Today, 40PlusGlow is more than a wellness brand—it's a growing community built on trust, education, encouragement, and intentional living. Whether you're taking your first step toward a healthier lifestyle or continuing to build lifelong wellness habits, we're honored to support your wellness journey and empower you to glow from the inside out.

Your wellness journey starts here. Let's glow together.

OUR MISSION

Empowering Healthier, More Confident Lives Every Day

At 40PlusGlow, our mission is to empower individuals to embrace healthier, more confident lives through trusted wellness education, premium self-care products, and an uplifting community. We believe true beauty begins from within, and we are committed to inspiring balance of the mind, body, and soul through intentional self-care and healthy living.

Through trusted products, wellness education, and genuine encouragement, we inspire healthier habits, greater confidence, and lasting well-being. We cultivate a supportive community where self-care is embraced, wellness is celebrated, and everyone is empowered to glow from the inside out.
